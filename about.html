ℹ️ About Student Toolkit

An independent educational project

Student Toolkit is a simple, independent website designed to give students quick access to useful study tools in one place.

What we provide

  • 🧮 Percentage, marks and CGPA calculators
  • ⏱️ A study timer for focused sessions
  • 📅 A weekly study timetable
  • 🔄 A unit converter
  • ⏳ An exam countdown
  • 📚 Links to official NCERT textbook resources

Independent project

Student Toolkit is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with NCERT, Google, Supabase, or any other third-party service mentioned on this website.

📚 NCERT resources: Student Toolkit links users to the official NCERT textbook portal rather than hosting or redistributing NCERT textbook files.

Our goal

The goal is to keep useful study utilities simple, accessible and free for students.
